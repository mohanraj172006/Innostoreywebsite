Mr. G. Mohanraj

Founder & Chairman

Mr. G. Mohanraj is the visionary force behind InnoStorey Innovations Pvt Ltd. With an unyielding passion for deep technology, innovation, and nation-building, he laid the foundation for InnoStorey to serve as a bridge between academic research, industrial application, and social impact.

Under his dynamic leadership, the company has grown into a multidisciplinary powerhouse focusing on Artificial Intelligence, Embedded Systems, Biomedical Engineering, and Smart City solutions.
